SUPPLEMENTARY FILE S1. SEARCH STRATEGIES USED FOR CORPUS CONSTRUCTION
**********************************************************************

This supplementary file reports the complete Scopus search strategies used 
to construct the Food Analytics corpus. The search design comprised one 
restrictive benchmark search (STRICT), three primary searches (MAIN-1 to 
MAIN-3), and three complementary searches (SUPPLEMENTARY-1 to 
SUPPLEMENTARY-3). The strategies were executed independently and their 
retrieved records were subsequently combined and deduplicated before 
application of the study eligibility criteria.

STRICT was designed to retrieve publications explicitly using established 
Food Analytics terminology and served as a high-precision reference search. 
MAIN-1, MAIN-2, and MAIN-3 represent alternative primary operationalizations 
of the field intended to increase retrieval robustness across terminology 
and indexing practices. SUPPLEMENTARY-1 to SUPPLEMENTARY-3 targeted adjacent 
application and methodological vocabularies that may represent Food Analytics 
research without explicitly using the field’s core terminology.

Database: Scopus
Search fields: As specified in each query below
Search date: May 2, 2026
Coverage: All years available in Scopus up to the search date
Document types: No restriction at search stage
Language: No restriction at search stage

Search results from the individual strategies were combined using unique Scopus 
identifiers and duplicate records were removed before screening.
