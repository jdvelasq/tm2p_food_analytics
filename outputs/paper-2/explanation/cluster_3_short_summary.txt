The agricultural, chemistry, and functional food analytics cluster is defined by the convergence of artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML), and advanced analytical chemistry approaches, leveraging data-centric, computational, and sensor-driven methodologies to address complex challenges across food, ingredient, and pharmaceutic domains.
