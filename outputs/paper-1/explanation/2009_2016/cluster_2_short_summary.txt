Thematic advances manifest in trends such as deep and convolutional neural networks displacing traditional hand-crafted features for image-based food classification, segmentation, and compositional analysis, with demonstrated efficacy in large-scale benchmarking and the practical domains of dietary assessment, quality inspection, obesity management, and personalized nutrition [UT 1782][UT 1619][UT 523][UT 851][UT 1612][UT 1704][UT 1694].
