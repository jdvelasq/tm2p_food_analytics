The neural, artificial, fuzzy, computer, temperature, and fractal methodologies at the heart of this cluster collectively redefine food analytics by integrating advanced computational intelligence with process and quality control in the food sector. This thematic family is distinguished by the development and application of neural networks, fuzzy logic, computer vision, and fractal theory, moving beyond the limitations of conventional microbiological and chemical methods toward automated, objective, and non-destructive systems for food safety and quality assurance [UT 1863][UT 1861][UT 1869]. However, challenges persist in the reliable formalization and integration of fuzzy, neural, and fractal models, particularly due to the complexity of sensory data, variable process conditions, noisy measurements, and the need for scalable, interpretable parameters suitable for practical deployment [UT 1855][UT 1867][UT 1876][UT 1851][UT 1869][UT 1863][UT 2070][UT 1861][UT 1866][UT 1857][UT 1860][UT 1858][UT 1874].
