Food analytics, as defined by this cluster, encompasses the convergence of technology-driven, user-centered systems that operationalize core food concepts—such as recipes, restaurants, supermarkets, and menus—through the lens of adaptive, intelligent, and context-aware digital platforms [UT 474][UT 796][UT 820][UT 1634][UT 389][UT 2017]. Central to this field is the deployment of ontology-based frameworks and unified food knowledge graphs, which bridge semantic and behavioral gaps to support interoperable, personalized, and health-oriented recommendations leveraging collaborative filtering and machine learning for both individual users and groups [UT 713][UT 684][UT 1714][UT 653][UT 1910][UT 415][UT 619][UT 744]. Recent trends reveal an expanding scope through deep neural networks, cross-modal retrieval, and real-time, context-sensitive interfacing across mobile, web, and wearable platforms, reflecting the sector’s commitment to integrating nutritional science, user behavioral data, and group eating dynamics for healthy and sustainable food choices [UT 561][UT 491][UT 612][UT 670][UT 590][UT 2027][UT 479][UT 785][UT 1999][UT 791][UT 1944][UT 648][UT 516][UT 652][UT 605][UT 506][UT 625][UT 2001][UT 770][UT 763][UT 488][UT 2025][UT 751][UT 641][UT 779][UT 811][UT 1775][UT 611][UT 489][UT 1959]. However, challenges persist in representing evolving user preferences, cultural dietary norms, and nutritional requirements, compounded by incomplete ontologies, sparsity in annotated data, cold start issues, and data heterogeneity that limit personalization and interoperability, especially in multi-user and cross-platform environments [UT 414][UT 796][UT 648][UT 516][UT 770][UT 1634][UT 474]. Research opportunities are rich in advancing integrative, multi-modal recommendation systems, building semantically robust ontologies, dynamic user models, and participatory platforms that engage diverse populations and stakeholders in real time [UT 796][UT 820][UT 713][UT 684][UT 1910][UT 415][UT 619][UT 1984][UT 2017][UT 648][UT 686][UT 612][UT 2027][UT 403][UT 611][UT 1634][UT 838][UT 1942][UT 2027][UT 670][UT 469][UT 652]. To enhance practical value, the field should prioritize interoperability via standardized ontological frameworks, co-design with stakeholders—including nutritionists, healthcare providers, and end users—and rigorous, longitudinal evaluation grounded in clinical, behavioral, and cultural outcomes, thus ensuring systems deliver actionable, personalized, and health-promoting recommendations across menus, recipes, and eating environments [UT 796][UT 820][UT 605][UT 648][UT 506][UT 474][UT 838][UT 1910][UT 1799][UT 590][UT 670][UT 612][UT 785][UT 489][UT 1775][UT 652][UT 662][UT 403][UT 2027].
