This thematic cluster in food analytics is defined by the rapid evolution and cross-pollination of neural and deep learning frameworks—chiefly convolutional neural networks (CNNs) combined with advanced computer vision and artificial intelligence—to automate and enhance tasks such as food recognition, classification, calorie estimation, and nutritional profiling using image-derived features like color, texture, and shape, as well as multimodal data streams [UT 1835][UT 1726][UT 414][UT 1614][UT 1737][UT 422][UT 1763][UT 1823][UT 1891][UT 1720][UT 1839][UT 1775][UT 1727][UT 1843][UT 1687][UT 1797][UT 1664][UT 1774][UT 1816][UT 1767][UT 1699][UT 1764]. The field trends toward increased use of large-scale, culturally diverse, and application-driven datasets—including crowdsourced and social media sources—while embracing mobile interfaces, real-time sensors, and user-centric recommendation systems tailored to address public health challenges like obesity and diabetes [UT 1686][UT 1942][UT 1656][UT 1650][UT 1668][UT 1595][UT 1833][UT 1798][UT 2027][UT 1695][UT 1635][UT 670][UT 1610][UT 1784][UT 791][UT 460][UT 605][UT 838][UT 422][UT 2001][UT 763][UT 811]. However, persistent challenges remain: the complex variability in food appearance, underrepresentation in datasets, and difficulty in generalizing to real-world, unstructured settings hamper robust recognition and classification, while the integration of text and sensory signals with visual data is still nascent [UT 1566][UT 1833][UT 1762][UT 1797][UT 1625][UT 1610][UT 1763][UT 1816][UT 1664][UT 414][UT 1573][UT 1891][UT 1839]. The need for interpretability, efficiency, and validation—especially in mobile and embedded deployments—adds further complexity, as does reconciling algorithmic precision with user satisfaction, personalization, and compliance to cultural or medical standards [UT 1668][UT 1798][UT 1729][UT 1706][UT 1651][UT 2017][UT 670][UT 469][UT 1703][UT 1774][UT 1575][UT 652].
