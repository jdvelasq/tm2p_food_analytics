This thematic cluster in food analytics encapsulates a multifaceted and rapidly evolving area shaped by the intersection of artificial intelligence, semantic modeling, natural language processing, and knowledge-driven methods for extracting, organizing, and contextualizing data related to food, safety, public health, and agriculture. Current research foregrounds the operationalization of semantic knowledge and explainable AI across diverse data modalities, including unstructured text, images, food composition records, and web-sourced information [UT 1885][UT 1415][UT 173][UT 1353][UT 470][UT 301][UT 784][UT 352][UT 201][UT 228][UT 30][UT 1408][UT 1303][UT 1483]. Notable trends include the shift from static databases to dynamic ontologies and food knowledge graphs, supporting interoperability and real-time decision-making for both public and personalized nutrition and safety interventions [UT 173][UT 1353][UT 525][UT 784][UT 3]. Advances in deep learning, semantic entity extraction, and multimodal integration are enabling automated recognition, categorization, and context-aware recommendations, while fostering more robust analytics in food safety, dietary planning, and supply chain optimization [UT 201][UT 202][UT 1441][UT 301][UT 485][UT 815][UT 622]. However, the field faces persistent challenges: fragmented, heterogeneous data sources; limited annotated corpora for food entities; complexities in harmonizing ontologies; difficulties in semantic alignment; and ongoing concerns around data privacy, transparency, and public trust [UT 783][UT 173][UT 784][UT 525][UT 30][UT 2016][UT 238][UT 219][UT 815][UT 560][UT 485]. Future directions emphasize not only expanding multi-modal, well-annotated datasets but also advancing context-aware, explainable models and privacy-preserving, interoperable knowledge platforms to underpin both scalable analytics and personalized health outcomes [UT 228][UT 305][UT 240][UT 522][UT 706][UT 1353][UT 271][UT 706][UT 783][UT 193][UT 1708]. Practical recommendations center on developing unified semantic standards, validated ontologies, and transparent AI models, while fostering cross-disciplinary collaboration, stakeholder co-design, and capacity building in digital infrastructure to ensure that the evolving ecosystem remains adaptive, equitable, and resilient. In synthesizing semantic, natural, and data-centric innovations, this cluster holds strategic promise for transformative advances in food safety, personalized nutrition, and public health at both individual and systemic scales [UT 110][UT 208][UT 643][UT 1551][UT 1708][UT 153].
