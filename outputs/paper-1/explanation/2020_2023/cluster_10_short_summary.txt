This thematic cluster centers on the integration of computational and data-driven methodologies to advance food analytics, with a pronounced focus on the multifaceted challenges and opportunities surrounding nutritional profiling, eating behaviors, cuisine and flavor diversity, personalized dietary recommendations, and equitable accessibility [UT 1498][UT 2115][UT 803][UT 1415][UT 173][UT 1441][UT 544][UT 1246][UT 2074][UT 1483][UT 701][UT 695][UT 534][UT 1281][UT 273][UT 1251][UT 352][UT 708][UT 1419][UT 1261][UT 1426][UT 1962][UT 519][UT 1413][UT 1485][UT 1955][UT 1961][UT 32][UT 1367][UT 305][UT 572][UT 609][UT 1776][UT 1674][UT 232][UT 65][UT 499][UT 1604][UT 1477][UT 2039][UT 692][UT 1777][UT 94][UT 1631][UT 2022][UT 1952][UT 176][UT 272][UT 1745][UT 510][UT 799][UT 379][UT 621][UT 417][UT 1597][UT 740][UT 1779][UT 1711][UT 633][UT 629][UT 767][UT 778]. The evolution of this field is marked by the rise of machine learning and natural language processing to support the classification and recommendation of foods across diverse datasets, enabling nuanced assessment of nutritional value, flavor, popularity, and culinary origins [UT 803][UT 1246][UT 609][UT 572][UT 519][UT 1413][UT 1955][UT 1961][UT 1498][UT 1415][UT 1631]. Despite methodological advances, the domain faces persistent challenges arising from the inherent diversity and complexity of food items, the variability in eating patterns, and the difficulties in robust classification and personalization caused by noisy, incomplete, or biased data and the lack of standardized ontologies [UT 352][UT 1415][UT 1498][UT 1246][UT 1251][UT 1483][UT 1674][UT 176]. These issues hinder the development of scalable, user-centered nutritional recommendation systems and exacerbate inequities in food accessibility. Opportunities lie in expanding multimodal, globally inclusive datasets, advancing explainable AI, and developing fairness-aware, interpretable, and adaptable machine learning frameworks to foster equitable, culturally relevant, and healthier food choices [UT 2115][UT 2074][UT 609][UT 1674][UT 305][UT 1441][UT 1955][UT 534][UT 701]. To address these challenges, future research should prioritize creating seamlessly integrated, cross-disciplinary systems that leverage critique-based interactions, automated feedback loops, and privacy-preserving solutions, while validating models at scale and incorporating user-controlled interfaces for individualized, actionable dietary guidance [UT 2022][UT 815][UT 510][UT 1690][UT 65][UT 1962][UT 305][UT 1477][UT 1777][UT 272][UT 1949][UT 1451][UT 767][UT 94][UT 173][UT 1711][UT 633][UT 32]. Practically, embedding transparent justifications and advanced recommendation mechanisms into digital and mobile systems—supported by standardized, comprehensive datasets and strategic partnerships with public health and regulatory bodies—will maximize the population-level impact, drive healthier, more diverse eating habits, and further democratize access to nutritious and culturally resonant foods [UT 803][UT 1498][UT 534][UT 1261][UT 1251][UT 1952][UT 1631][UT 1690][UT 1674][UT 609][UT 1419][UT 1441][UT 1246][UT 767][UT 778][UT 633][UT 815][UT 2022][UT 544][UT 32][UT 94][UT 176][UT 271][UT 629][UT 1261][UT 273][UT 379][UT 621][UT 767][UT 2029][UT 2039][UT 1751][UT 1483][UT 305][UT 2115][UT 2074][UT 692][UT 1777][UT 1477][UT 1881].
