The thematic family is characterized by the application of advanced computational techniques for food and calorie recognition, nutrient profiling, dietary prediction, and the personalization of healthy, balanced diets, leveraging rich, multimodal data sources such as health records, food labels, user images, and microbiome data [UT 1390][UT 1467][UT 139][UT 544][UT 547][UT 1246][UT 1483][UT 534][UT 1326][UT 1335][UT 1537][UT 695][UT 1304]. Current trends reflect rapid methodological diversification, with deep learning and recommender systems driving personalized dietary interventions and increasing use of knowledge graphs, clustering approaches like k-means, and real-time IoT-enabled data streams [UT 1958][UT 1391][UT 2089][UT 1545][UT 229][UT 353][UT 609][UT 1421][UT 1241][UT 1413][UT 638][UT 661][UT 471][UT 781].
