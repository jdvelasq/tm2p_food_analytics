Situated at the dynamic intersection of advanced digital technologies, artificial intelligence, and data-driven analytics, this cluster defines a multidisciplinary paradigm in food analytics that spans the entire agri-food system—from primary agricultural production to consumer engagement and population health outcomes [UT 241][UT 1343][UT 363][UT 831][UT 1467][UT 1530]. Recent trends underscore the rapid proliferation of machine learning, drone-based monitoring, genetic algorithms, and big data methodologies, transforming agri-food supply chains into digitized, interoperable, and transparent systems emblematic of Agri-Food 4.0 and Agriculture 4.0 [UT 1924][UT 1890][UT 1422][UT 587][UT 1917]. Core thematic foci include individualized dietary recommendations powered by the integration of genetic, demographic, and income data; algorithmic marketing systems; and real-time consumer health analytics that reflect evolving population needs and market behaviors [UT 1958][UT 519][UT 55][UT 227][UT 772][UT 1395]. However, significant challenges persist: the heterogeneity and fragmentation of digital tools, stakeholder skepticism, organizational readiness gaps, and regulatory complexity often impede scalable adoption and generalizability, particularly given the structural diversity and fluctuating population and income landscapes within agri-food sectors [UT 1530][UT 1924][UT 227][UT 1582][UT 519][UT 1958][UT 1343][UT 1397][UT 1366][UT 290][UT 106]. Methodologically, achieving robust, explainable, and privacy-preserving analytics across varied and noisy datasets, while ensuring algorithmic transparency and equitable inclusion of underrepresented consumer populations, remains a critical barrier [UT 1885][UT 1343][UT 567][UT 215][UT 55]. Research opportunities lie in integrative AI-model development—including federated learning, hybrid modeling, and ontological harmonization—to bolster food safety, traceability, and personalized nutrition, with emphasis on trust, digital literacy, and context-sensitive consumer engagement [UT 1366][UT 1397][UT 1549][UT 1411][UT 1988][UT 296][UT 587][UT 783][UT 685][UT 1627][UT 1530][UT 707][UT 363][UT 1581][UT 831][UT 1317][UT 329][UT 1701][UT 337]. Practically, advancing this cluster’s impact requires participatory, user-centered co-design frameworks prioritizing modular, cost-sensitive AI and IoT solutions, harmonized data infrastructures, and ongoing stakeholder education to overcome adoption and equity barriers, thereby enabling scalable, resilient, and trustworthy innovations across the global food ecosystem [UT 1530][UT 363][UT 1366][UT 56][UT 4][UT 1586][UT 783][UT 1643][UT 337][UT 1917][UT 1343][UT 1581][UT 374][UT 1530][UT 1924][UT 290][UT 1395][UT 685][UT 1627][UT 833][UT 538][UT 1448][UT 169][UT 2003][UT 2032].
